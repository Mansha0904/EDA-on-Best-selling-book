Best Selling Books Analysis (EDA)


Description
This project investigates the trends and characteristics of Amazon's top-selling books from 2023 to 2025. By analyzing factors such as genre popularity, pricing strategies, and author consistency, this EDA aims to uncover what makes a book "sticky" in the bestseller lists over multiple years. The analysis involves cleaning complex datasets, handling currency conversions, and merging yearly snapshots to track the performance of titles like Atomic Habits and the Empyrean series.

Key Findings:
-Perennial Bestsellers: Identified "Evergreen" titles that remained in the top rankings across all three years (2023–2025), showing the longevity of the Self-Improvement genre.
-Genre Trends: Analyzed the dominance of Fiction & Action & Adventure and Biographies, noting how major cultural events (like celebrity memoir releases) shift market share.
-Review Impact: Discovered a high correlation between massive review counts (300k+) and sustained ranking, even when prices fluctuated.
-Format Preferences: Compared the prevalence of Hardcover vs. Paperback formats, highlighting which categories favor premium physical editions over budget-friendly ones.

Technologies Used:
Pandas: For multi-year data merging and cleaning.
NumPy: For statistical analysis of price and rating distributions.
Matplotlib & Seaborn: For visualizing the "ranking journey" of books over time.
